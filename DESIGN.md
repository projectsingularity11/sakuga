# Sakuga — midnight cinema

The redesign follows the user's references: Miruro's artwork density and anime browsing, Unseen's editorial art direction, and Lusion's immersive first viewport. Product truth stays in PRODUCT.md. The visual metaphor is a late-night screening room, with a hand-inked eye / play-frame brand mark representing the moment animation comes alive.

Deep charcoal #101211 canvas; forest #283b31 composition surface; warm ivory #f3f2e9 text; muted sage #afb6b0 secondary text; persimmon #ef8762 accents. DM Sans medium and bold for navigation and confident headings; Newsreader italic for emotional display emphasis. Fonts are self-hosted.

Homepage: original panoramic anime city art, left-aligned display and direct catalog action, compact search dock, six actual catalog covers, a layered collection composition, three image-led genre links, and a simple invitation. Never show fake catalog counts, scores or personal activity. Artwork and metadata have attribution. Covers link to real public detail routes. No video autoplay or continuous animation. Hover artwork scale and reveal are disabled with reduced motion.

Public discovery and detail: Operate mode. Server-side search, pagination, filters, explicit source labels and unavailable metadata. The catalog belongs to Supabase; the ten-item homepage selection is a curated showcase, never the production database. Public browse does not require an account; library changes do.

Workspace: Operate mode. Retain working forms, ownership, library views, collection tools and preferences. Dark by default; explicit saved light/dark/system preferences take precedence. Sidebar and browsing use the same brand and tokens. Mobile has two-column covers and stacked genre tiles with touch targets.

Logo: original SVG linework, an imperfect hand-inked eye enclosing a play shape, with animation action lines. This is a geometric identity mark, not an illustrated scene. Maintain legibility at navigation scale.

Validate desktop and mobile together. Any deployment or catalog completeness claim requires remote evidence.

## Discovery upgrade

Homepage is now Operate mode: a compact real featured anime with safe synopsis, direct detail/list actions, and independent popular, release-year and upcoming feeds. The original marketing scene and promotional collection mockups are no longer the page structure. Navigation exposes Discover, My list and shared live search. Neutral charcoal surfaces replace the green canvas; a single persimmon accent marks actions and selected navigation. Cards have stable 2:3 covers, two-line titles, release metadata and separate keyboard-accessible list links.

The HD requirement is enforced on original dimensions: minimum 720×1080, target 1080×1620. Lower-quality/unverified images use an explicit fallback; no artificial enlargement or invented HD labels. This can leave many posters unavailable until qualifying source images exist. Current sources do not guarantee the required resolution for every work.
