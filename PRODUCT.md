# Sakuga

A personal anime sanctuary: discovery, transparent source ratings, private libraries and collections. Next.js App Router, Supabase Postgres/Auth, Stripe hosted one-time checkout, Vercel deployment. No fabricated catalog or ratings; 10,000 eligible distinct works is a launch gate. Pornography excluded; ambiguous records quarantined; mature themes alone do not imply pornography. Credentials, provider commercial permission and actual deployment are external prerequisites.
